Before you start

Four things that break the run.

Sign in before you startThe session expires after roughly an hour idle.
Confirm the deck is LandscapeOrientation persists between sessions; only a reload reverts it.
Leave both outline cards unapprovedStages 3 and 5 depend on them. Approving removes the card.
Stay out of Organization → ErrorsIt lists open reports org-wide. The Self-Healing tab covers the same ground in context.

If they ask

The four questions that come up, and the honest answer.

Can it reach our internal systems?api_connector_tool makes approved external HTTP calls and is in the library. It is configured on zero workstreams today. F90
What does it cost?Metered per workstream. The reference workstream: 6.8M tokens over 25 sessions, $21.97 estimated, broken down by model. F87
Who can see my work?A conversation is private to the people granted access. Separately, the workstream template is organisation-wide by default. F57
Which model is it using?Chosen per workstream before anything is spent, limited to the four models the organisation has approved. An unapproved model is refused, not substituted. F86
